# EXAMPLE 1:FAKE BANK MESSAGE;
"Your bank account has been locked. Click this link to verify your account immediately."
# Warning signs:
The message creates urgency
The link may lead to a fake banking website
It may ask for passwords or banking details.
# how to stay safe :Contact your bank using its official phone number or website.
# EXAMPLE2:FAKE UNIVERSITY EMAIL;
"Your university account will be suspended today. Enter your username and password to keep it active."
# Warning sign:
It threatens to suspend the account.
It asks for login details through an unexpected link.
# How to stay safe: vist the official university website directly and contact IT support if needed.
# EXAMPLE2:FAKE PRIZE NOTIFICATION;
"Congratulations! you have won a free smartphone. Pay a small fee to claim your prize".
# Warning signs:
You may not have entered any competition.
It asks for money before giving you the prize.
It may contain suspicious links.
# How to stay safe: verify the competition through the organisation's official channels.
# CONCLUSION
Always check the sender , verify unexpected requests and avoid suspicious links. Never share passwords or verification codes through untrusted messages.
# REMEMBER:STOP,CHECK AND VERIFY BEFORE YOU CLICK.
