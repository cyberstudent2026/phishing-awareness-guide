# WHAT IS PHISHING?
Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals pretend to be trusted people or organisations in order to trick users into revealing sensitive information, clicking malicious links or opening unsafe attachments
# COMMON WARNING SIGNS OF PHISHING ATTACKS
Suspicious links: Links may lead to fake websites designed to steal login details of people.
Unexpected attachments: Attachments may contain malware that harms devices or steal information.
Urgent messages: Be careful and safe with unexpected emails, messages and unknown attachments
Request for passwords: Never share your passwords or verification codes through emails or messages.
# HOW TO STAY SAFE
Check the senders full email address.
Avoid clicking unexpected links.
Visit official websites by typing their addresses into your browser.
Never share passwords or codes with anyone
Enable multi-factor authentication
Report suspicious messages to your institution's IT department.
# CONCLUSION
Recognizing phishing warnings signs helps protect personal information, university accounts and computer systems. Pause and verify every message before taking action.
